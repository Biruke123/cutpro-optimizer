No. The setup wizard installs everything for you. Once installed, you just double-click the CutPro shortcut on your Desktop and it starts the server, opens the dashboard, and waits for your SketchUp model — all automatically.
Open your SketchUp model, select any face of a part, and paint it blue. Then run Plugins → CutPro → Scatter & Send. CutPro will detect the blue face and calculate the veneer length based on the part type (doors and drawers get all 4 edges, shelves and side panels get just the front edge).
CutPro filters out very small parts (under 30 mm), solid pieces thicker than 50 mm, and any shape larger than 3 meters. If your part falls outside those ranges, it's skipped on purpose because it isn't a real panel.
Open Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find CutPro v3.8, and click Uninstall. That removes the backend, the SketchUp plugin, and the desktop shortcut.
No. Everything runs on your machine. The backend server runs at localhost:5000 and never sends your parts to any external server. User accounts and history are stored locally.
